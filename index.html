Tinghui Zhou

Director, Foundation AI  ·  Roblox

I am a research lead at Roblox's Foundation AI group. Our research spans 3D foundation models, video world models, and agentic game generation.

Previously, I founded Sway, an AI video generation startup (acquired by Roblox) that made it to #3 overall on the App Store — back before video models even “worked.”

I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Alexei (Alyosha) Efros during my Ph.D. at UC Berkeley.
